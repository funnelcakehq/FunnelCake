Before you ask

The questions owners actually ask us.

How much does it cost? +

Every system is built around your business, so there's no one-size price. Most full builds start around [SWAP: your starting price], and smaller scopes cost less. After the first call you get a fixed quote in writing, so you know the exact number before you commit to anything. No surprise invoices later.

I've worked with an agency before and it didn't go well. How are you different? +

The usual agency story is that your work gets handed to a junior, the pieces never connect, and the results disappear the day you stop paying. We build one connected system instead of scattered tasks, hand you full ownership with a playbook, and let you decide whether to keep us on. If you ever walk away, the system stays with you and keeps working.

Do I have to keep you on a retainer? +

No. We build the system, automate it, and teach you how it runs. From there you can keep the whole setup and run it yourself, or have us run and improve it for you month to month. Completely your choice, no lock-in either way.

What if I don't have a brand or website yet? +

That's the most common place we start, and it's easier this way. We build your identity first, then your website and presence, then the automation on top. You don't need anything ready beforehand. If you already have some pieces, we keep what's worth keeping and rebuild the rest.

How long until the system is live? +

It depends on what already exists. We build in the right order, identity first, then your website and presence, then the automation, so nothing public is ever half-finished. You'll get a clear timeline in your blueprint after the first call.

How involved do I need to be? +

One short check-in a week, around fifteen minutes. We bring decisions to you already thought through, so you're approving rather than figuring things out. Beyond that, you stay focused on your actual work.

What happens after handover? +

You get a simple, step-by-step playbook and a full walkthrough of every part of your system. The automations keep capturing and following up with new enquiries on their own. If anything breaks in the first month, we fix it free.

Who isn't this a fit for? +

If you want to approve every single post, you're after a quick one-off task, or you expect everything to change in week one, we're probably not your team. We work best with owners ready to hand the digital side over and let a system do its job.
