Before you ask

The questions owners actually ask.

What does it cost?

Every system is built around your business, so there is no one size price. Smaller scopes cost less than full builds. After the first call you get a fixed quote in writing, so you know the exact number before you commit. No surprise invoices later.

I have been burned by an agency before.

Most of that story runs the same way. Your work goes to a junior, the pieces never connect, and everything stops the day you stop paying. You get the same small team from the first call to handover, one connected system rather than scattered tasks, and full ownership at the end.

Do I have to stay on a retainer?

No. We build it, automate it and teach you how it runs. From there you can keep the whole setup and run it yourself, or have us run it month to month. No lock in either way.

I do not have a brand or website yet.

That is the easiest place to start. We build identity first, then the website and presence, then the follow ups on top. If you already have pieces worth keeping, we keep them and rebuild the rest.

How much of my time does this take?

About fifteen minutes a week. We bring decisions to you already thought through, so you are approving rather than working things out. Beyond that you stay on your actual job.

One call and it is handled.

Twenty minutes. We show you the gaps in what you have, whether or not you hire us.
