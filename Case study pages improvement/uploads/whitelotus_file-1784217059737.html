Two ways to move. One place to belong.

White Lotus is a quiet studio in Abu Shagara, made for women and children. Reformer Pilates and classical yoga sit side by side here. Come for the reformer — to sculpt strength, correct posture, or recover from injury. Come for yoga — to move with breath and find a stiller mind. Whether you train privately or in an intimate group, the attention is the same: close, considered, and entirely yours.
