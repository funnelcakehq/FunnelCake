The problem

Where they started

White Lotus had everything a local business dreams of — 200+ five-star Google reviews, a loyal reformer Pilates and private yoga clientele, and a genuine point of difference in the classical Indian tradition. What it had online: almost nothing. No website, no managed channels, no way for “reformer pilates Sharjah” searches to find them.

This is our longest-running retainer, and the clearest picture of what FunnelCake actually does: not a website project, but a business’s complete digital visibility — established from zero and run month after month.

The full stack, from zero

Branding, a hand-coded search-first website, Instagram, YouTube, WhatsApp funnel and Google Business Profile — every channel established and connected by one team.

Google Business as the engine

For a local studio, the GBP listing is the storefront. We run it: services, posts, review flow, Q&A — built around their 200+ five-star reviews.

Google Ads on live searches

Paid capture on high-intent local searches — reformer Pilates, private yoga — pointed at fast pages built to convert, not a generic homepage.

Schema-first website

HealthClub + FAQ structured data with services, hours, geo and ratings — so search engines read the studio’s credentials, not just visitors.
