The problem

Where they started

A luxury real estate agent in the Phoenix metro needed more than a brochure — the site had to do the job: surface live listings, capture seller leads through valuations, and answer the fifty questions every buyer asks before they ever call an agent.

US real estate sites live and die on their MLS integration. Static “featured homes” go stale in a week; this one pulls live regional listings — open houses, price changes, days on market — automatically.

What we built

Live IDX / MLS integration

Real listings from the regional MLS feed — new properties, open house dates and status changes appear without anyone touching the site.

Two-sided lead capture

Buyers get search and neighbourhood guides; sellers get a home-valuation / cash-offer funnel. Two audiences, two funnels, one site.

Mortgage calculator + guides

Buyer’s guide, seller’s guide and a payment calculator keep prospects on-site through the research phase instead of bouncing to Zillow.

A 14-question FAQ engine

Pre-approval vs pre-qualification, PMI, earnest money, contingencies — the whole first-call conversation, answered in advance.
