The problem

Where they started

Tao of Touch is a trauma-informed bodywork and somatics school — Thai massage, Chi Nei Tsang, advanced bodywork — with 330+ students trained, ISO-compliant certifications, and a founder who teaches at international festivals. Until Phase 1, all of that lived in Instagram bios and word of mouth.

Phase 1 gave the school a central home: courses as structured pages, video testimonials, an FAQ that handles the questions every prospective student asks, and WhatsApp as the enquiry channel. Phase 2 — e-commerce for course bookings — is scoped and next.

Courses as products

Five trainings — Thai Massage, Advanced Bodywork, Chi Nei Tsang, Karsai Nei Tsang, Access Bars — each with its own page and clear positioning.

Credibility stack

ISO 9001 / 21001-compliant certification messaging, video testimonials, and festival presence — the trust signals a touch-based practice lives or dies on.

FAQ that pre-sells

Answers to what ‘trauma aware’ actually means, who the trainings are for, and what certification students receive — objections handled before the DM.

Ad campaigns, run properly

Paid campaigns driving training enquiries — targeted at practitioners and embodiment audiences, pointed at course pages, not a homepage.

Phase 2 scoped

WooCommerce + Razorpay course checkout is speced and ready — turning enquiries into direct bookings.
